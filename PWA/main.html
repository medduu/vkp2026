Hello World PWA

This is a simple Progressive Web App built as a learning exercise. It can be installed to your home screen, loads instantly on repeat visits thanks to a service worker, and keeps working even when you lose your internet connection.

A PWA combines the reach of the web with some of the perks of a native app: no app store, no install step beyond "add to home screen", and it still runs behind the scenes with a background script called a service worker.

Try it yourself: open this page once while online, then turn on airplane mode and reload. The page, its styling, this background image, and the custom font should all still load.
